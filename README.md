# Me²

## Problem Statement
People often make their worst decisions — or freeze entirely — in emotionally 
overwhelming moments, disconnected from the evidence of their own resilience 
and good judgment. Generic advice doesn't help because it isn't grounded in 
their life.

## Solution
A personal reflection companion that helps users navigate difficult decisions 
by drawing on their own remembered moments of strength and clarity — not by 
predicting outcomes, but by reconnecting them with patterns from their own 
past choices.
